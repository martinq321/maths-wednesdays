About this strategy

The complete strategy averages 4.341049 guesses and takes at most five, including the final correct guess, when all 1,296 secrets are equally likely. Its opening uses a pair and two different colours.

This page implements Koyama and Lai’s published decision table with their five-guess modification. All 1,296 secrets were checked: 5,626 guesses in total, with none taking more than five.

Only histories whose visible clues leave exactly one answer are used here. There are 14 puzzles solved on guess 3, 415 on guess 4 and 547 on guess 5. The average above describes the full strategy, not this filtered puzzle collection. Guess count is not a human difficulty rating.

The game hints, strategy tree and general puzzle page now use this same strategy on the classic board. This strategy’s guarantee is for six colours and four holes only.

Koyama & Lai, An Optimal Mastermind Strategy (1993) — Table 1 and the modification K in section 3.
