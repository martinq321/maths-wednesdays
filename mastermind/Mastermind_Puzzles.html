How puzzles are chosen

These puzzles follow the same strategy as the game hints and the strategy tree. The selected length is the strategy’s actual number of guesses, including the final correct code. It is not a rating of human difficulty.

Smaller states use minimax or stored policies. Larger states use sampled candidate guesses; this is not a globally optimal solver. A limited search may not find your requested length.

All displayed clues together must determine exactly one code. Ambiguous histories are always rejected. A one-guess puzzle has no clues and is available only when the board has one colour.

The last feedback row is always shown. The hidden final code’s all-black feedback appears only when you reveal the solution.
